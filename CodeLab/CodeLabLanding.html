Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the CodeLab programme.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. The programme is designed to take students from absolute zero coding knowledge to a job-ready level through a structured path.

How long is the programme?

A minimum of 6 months, and only once every lesson and task is complete with a passing grade average.

Is the certificate verifiable?

Yes. Anyone can check a certificate's authenticity using the "Verify a certificate" tool inside the CodeLab dashboard.

Can I switch courses after signing up?

Not directly from your dashboard yet — reach out to an admin and they can move you to a different course.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled securely through Paystack, supporting cards and bank transfer in Naira.
