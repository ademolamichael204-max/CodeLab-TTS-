Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about ordering with Savora.

What areas do you deliver to?

Delivery is available within the city, with a flat delivery fee added at checkout. Pickup is always free.

How do I track my order?

Once you log in, your dashboard shows a live progress bar for every order — received, preparing, ready, completed.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled securely through Paystack — cards and bank transfer in Naira.

Can I cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible — orders can be cancelled by our staff before preparation begins.
